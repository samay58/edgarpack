# Thread Drafts

This folder holds publishable thread drafts and short notes derived from
EdgarPack outputs. They are useful context, but they are not canonical docs,
implementation plans, or source ledgers.

When a thread becomes a larger research project, promote it into a durable
bundle under `reports/<slug>/` with a `README.md`, evidence ledger, and source
tables.

When a thread may need public visuals, consult `docs/THREAD_VISUALS.md` before
drafting the final beats. The Cerebras portrait filing series in
`assets/thread-visual-reference/cerebras-filing-series-2026/` is the current
quality bar for social cards.
